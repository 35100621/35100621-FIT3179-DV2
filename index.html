Act I · The national divide

From a distance, the system appears to grow.

Public hospital beds grew 15.44% between 2015 and 2024, while population grew 9.19%. The national gain is real, yet seven of sixteen states and territories saw their public-bed rate fall. Growth alone establishes neither equal distribution nor adequate access.

Act III · The hidden private sector

Now reveal the beds the first map left out.

Private capacity does not simply add more of the same. It clusters, stretches the distance between states, and can reorder the national picture.

The finding is not a number

More beds do not necessarily mean equal access, available capacity, or sufficient care for the future.

Bed counts are a starting point—not a verdict on access or quality. Geography, provider mix, occupancy, activity, and demography each redraw the picture.

Sources & methodologyObserved MOH and DOSM data through 2024. Public beds include special institutions; private beds cover licensed hospitals. Occupancy is annual and MOH-only. Admissions have incomplete reporting. Ageing is context, not a demand forecast.
