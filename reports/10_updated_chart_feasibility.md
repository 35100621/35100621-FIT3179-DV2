### Recommended selection, not a quota

Start with the national growth story, one detailed public trend, one public/private comparison, one geographic rate view, annual MOH occupancy, carefully labelled admission mix, an age view and a workforce context view. Conditional hospital points and a private-capacity symbol map can add geographical information after data repair. Retain rank and waterfall views only if they add information beyond the chosen comparisons. Having ten possible idioms does not justify using every one.

A 15–17 view page is a design target, not a validated outcome of this task. The present evidence supports more analytical dimensions, but forcing a fourth map or repeated rankings would weaken the story. Obtain rubric confirmation and evaluate a small wireframe before promising exact totals. All eventual figures need author, publication date, actual reporting period, source URL, units, scope, denominator and concise limitations.
